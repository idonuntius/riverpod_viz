## 0.1.0

- Initial release.
- `RiverpodVizObserver` — a `ProviderObserver` that posts lifecycle events (`add`, `update`, `dispose`, `error`) via `dart:developer.postEvent`.
- DevTools extension with Gantt-chart-style timeline visualization.
- Support for family providers with argument display.
- Auto-scroll to latest events with manual scroll override.
- Clear timeline with automatic re-add of alive providers.
